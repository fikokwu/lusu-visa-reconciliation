Receipts

Drop receipts in your folder

Save photos or PDFs to your receipts folder as you go. Open it from Receipts folder ↗ or the link on your Dashboard.

Any file name, any subfolder. The app reads what's printed on the receipt.

Several receipts in one scan is fine. Each is matched to its own charge.

When the amount matches a charge exactly, the receipt is attached for you: when you open your reconciliation, every hour, or when you click Scan Drive.
