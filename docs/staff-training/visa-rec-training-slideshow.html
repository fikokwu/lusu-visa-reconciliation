Receipts

Drop receipts in your folder

Save photos or PDFs to your receipts folder as you go. Open it from Receipts folder ↗ or the link on your Dashboard.

Any file name, any subfolder. The app reads what's printed on the receipt.

Several receipts in one scan is fine. Each is matched to its own charge.

When the amount matches a charge exactly, the receipt is attached for you: when you open your reconciliation, every hour, or when you click Scan Drive.

Receipts

Receipts to review

Receipts that couldn't be attached for you are listed under your transactions.

Suggested match: close but not exact, like a tip or US dollars. Check the pre-selected charge, then click Match.

No match found or can't be read: pick the charge yourself, then click Match.

No file in your folder? Click Attach on the line and upload a PDF, JPG or PNG.

Finishing up

Submit for review

Click Submit for review once every purchase has a code and a receipt. If anything is missing, you get a list of what to fix.

After you submit, the reconciliation is locked. You can still view it.

Follow its status on your Dashboard.

What each status means
DraftYou're still working on it.
SubmittedWaiting for Finance to review.
Ed ReviewFinance approved it. Waiting for the Executive Director.
ApprovedDone. Filed in Approved Visa Recs, and you get an email.
Sent BackChanges needed. The notes are in your email.

Finishing up

Sent back? Fix and resubmit

Finance or the ED can send a reconciliation back. You get an email with their notes.

On your Dashboard it shows Sent Back. Click Fix & resubmit.

Make the changes, then click Submit for review again.
